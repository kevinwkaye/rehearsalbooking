# Administrator guide

## First-time setup

Follow `DEPLOYMENT.md` to promote exactly one registered account to administrator using the Supabase SQL Editor. Never paste the SQL into a public app or share the privileged database credentials.

## New bands

Bands register themselves with their contact name, email, band name, member count and password. The account remains **pending** until approved. Verify the contact and band independently before approval, because this no-SMTP MVP does not confirm email ownership.

Open **Admin** and choose **Approve** or **Reject**. Approved bands can immediately see the calendar and book dates. Each band has a single shared email/password account; individual musicians are not separate app users.

## Colours, contact and membership

Select **Edit band** to change the displayed name, contact name, member count and calendar colour. Member count is informational; it does not require voting. The account's sign-in email is not editable through this panel: changing Auth credentials requires a secure administrator process through Supabase.

## Booking rules

A confirmed booking reserves the **entire day**. A start time is displayed for convenience, but another band cannot book that day even at a different time. The database enforces one confirmed booking per date. For a requested clash, speak to the bands outside the app, then reschedule or cancel the existing booking before assigning the date to another band.

You can open any booking from the calendar and edit or cancel it. All changes appear to the other bands and are recorded in the audit database.

## Account access and recovery

If a band forgets its password, click **Recovery link** in the Admin panel, confirm the request, copy the time-sensitive link, verify the person requesting it and send it from `bunkface_booking_boutique@proton.me`. Never share it in public chats. Links can only be generated by the administrator via a trusted server function.

Use **Deactivate** to block an account immediately, then **Reactivate** if needed. Do not share the administrator login with band members.

## Notifications

The app has an in-app notification inbox. Users may enable phone push notifications in the calendar view; they must give browser notification permission. For iPhones, install the app to the Home Screen first. Each device must enable notifications individually, even if it shares a band login.
