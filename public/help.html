How it works.

One room. A few bands. Everyone knows when they're playing. Here's how to get set up, book a date and keep the calendar straight.

01 Getting in

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap New here? Create an account.
  2. Enter your name and email, then tick every band you play in. You can select more than one.
  3. Create a password with at least 10 characters and submit.
  4. The administrator approves the account once. Until then, you can't book the room.
  5. Once approved, sign in with your own email address and password.

Only the administrator can change your band assignments after you've registered. Ask them if you join or leave a band.

02 Booking a practice

  1. Open the Calendar and choose a free date. Swipe sideways to move through months on mobile, or use the arrows.
  2. Choose a start time. If you're in multiple bands, choose which band you're booking for.
  3. Tap Request practice.

One confirmed booking per day. The start time is for information; confirming a rehearsal reserves the whole day for that band.

Past dates can't be booked. A pending rehearsal request doesn't reserve a date: another band's request could be confirmed first.

Need to move or cancel?

Open your band's confirmed date in the calendar to update the start time or move the booking, or choose Cancel this practice. You can add an optional reason of up to 200 characters. Everyone approved in your band receives a cancellation notice, including you. Other bands are told the room is available, without your private reason. You can only manage rehearsals for your assigned bands. Admin can also manage bookings.

03 Band confirmations

Only one approved member registered for the band? Your rehearsal is confirmed straight away.

Two or more approved members? Everyone currently registered and approved in that band must confirm. The person who requested it counts as the first confirmation.

  1. Bandmates get an in-app notice and, if enabled, a phone alert.
  2. Open Notifications using the bell, find the rehearsal and choose Confirm or Decline. Declining opens a confirmation with an optional reason (up to 200 characters). Other approved band members are notified that the request was cancelled, with your reason if supplied.
  3. When everyone confirms, the booking appears as a confirmed calendar date.

If someone declines, the request ends and the room stays available. If band membership changes while a request is pending, the band needs to submit a fresh request.

04 Notifications

The bell in the top bar opens notifications. Opening it marks existing messages as read. It does not automatically confirm a rehearsal. You must still tap Confirm or Decline.

Use Clear notifications beside the phone alert controls to remove old in-app messages after confirming. This does not affect pending practice confirmations or turn off phone alerts.

For phone alerts, open the bell, then choose Enable phone alerts and allow notifications in your device's settings.

  • Android: open the site in Chrome, allow notifications, and optionally add the app to your Home screen.
  • iPhone: in Safari, add the site to your Home Screen first. Open the installed app and enable alerts there.

Phone alerts are optional. You can still check rehearsal confirmations through the app. If you move to a new website address, you may need to enable alerts again.

05 Running the room

The Admin section is only visible to the administrator.

  • Approvals: the Pending approvals counter takes you to accounts waiting to join. Review the selected bands, then approve the account.
  • Bands: create bands, edit names, choose a colour and enter an expected member count. The expected count is for reference, not a booking threshold.
  • Members: use Manage bands to add or remove band assignments. At least one band must remain assigned when saving.
  • Accounts: deactivate, reactivate, reset a member's password or delete an account. Deleting an account preserves historical booking records.
  • Activity: Recent activity shows an audit trail of account and booking actions.

The admin only votes on a rehearsal when they're a member of the band requesting it. They aren't an extra approver for everyone else.

06 Stuck?

Forgot your password?

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen, enter your registered email address and follow the reset link. Check your junk folder if it doesn't arrive.

Account awaiting approval or deactivated?

Only the administrator can approve or reactivate accounts. Contact the person running the room if your account or band membership needs changing.

Can't request a practice?

Check that you're signed in, your account is approved, you're assigned to the band, the date is available and you're connected to the internet.
