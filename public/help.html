THE QUICK GUIDE

How it works.

All you need to know to get the band in the room.

01 Join your band

Choose New here? Create an account, enter your details and select your band (or bands). Once your account is approved, you're in.

02 Book a practice

Tap an available future date, choose your start time and tap Request practice. If you're in more than one band, choose which one you're booking for.

Once everyone in your band confirms, the date is booked. If you're the only member registered, it's booked straight away. Each booking reserves the whole day.

03 Confirm or decline

Tap the bell to see requests from your band. Choose Confirm or Decline. If anyone declines, the request is cancelled and bandmates are notified. You can add an optional reason.

04 Change of plans?

Tap your band's booked date to move, edit or cancel the practice. You can add an optional reason when cancelling. Your bandmates will be notified.

05 Notifications & help

Use the bell to enable phone alerts or clear old messages. Clearing notifications does not affect pending practice confirmations or turn off phone alerts.

Forgot your password? Select Forgot password? when signing in. For account or band problems, ask whoever runs the room.
